# THE UNOWNABLE CENTER: Schema

This repository holds one book, THE UNOWNABLE CENTER, by A.M. Covino (Ammon M. Covino),
Covino Press, Austin, Texas, and the working wiki its editor steward keeps over it.

**This book is not part of THE ALPHA-OMEGA WORKS.** Ammon ruled on 30 September 2026 that it is
a separate book and a separate sale, not in the four-book corpus and not in the omnibus. The
series has its own wiki in the private repo `ammoncovino/alpha-omega-wiki`. Nothing here is
copied into that wiki, and nothing from the series is claimed for this book.

The steward keeps the wiki. Ammon rules. Nobody else rules.


## Link discipline (Obsidian)

Same rules as the series wiki.

1. **Filenames are globally unique.** Obsidian resolves by filename across folders.
2. **Never write a link you do not also create.** No empty stubs.
3. **Display aliases for grammar:** `[[Exposure|exposed]]`.
4. **No colons, slashes, or `*?"<>|` in filenames.** Section titles that carry a colon use a comma.
5. Run `bash tools/lint-links.sh` before ending any session and fix every break.

## What the wiki may and may not do

The wiki **reads and counts. It does not edit the book.** Until Ammon closes the scope rulings
(handoff, section 7, steps 1 and 2), no section is rewritten, proposed, or merged here.

- **Describe; do not judge.** A section page says what the section claims, where, and what it
  points to. It does not say whether the claim is right. That is the rhetoric pass and the
  hedge register, later, under Ammon's ruling.
- **Quote, do not paraphrase into new claims.** Where a page says the manuscript claims
  something, it quotes or cites the line.
- **No theology beyond the manuscript.** The Jesus and Logos material is the manuscript's
  own words. Do not summarize it into a position, and do not add a bridge to THE GLASS.
- **Findings are not fixes.** A finding names the line and the problem and stops. It goes to
  Ammon or to the section owner.
- **Steward residue is reported, not removed.** Recovery comments, "Recovered structural
  notes:" labels, and the like stay in `manuscript/` until a ruled build strips them.

## House style

From the handoff, section 3.6. It applies to every page Ammon reads.

- No contractions. No em dashes. American spelling.
- Write every title in full. **The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Homo Sapiens**, all nine words,
  every time. Filenames and slugs are the only exception.
- Byline `A.M. Covino`, no space. The repo README writes `A. M. Covino`; that is a known defect,
  reported, not fixed here.
- Plain language. When a term comes up, say what it is in one line.
- Tables and callouts over prose walls: `> [!note]`, `> [!warning]`, `> [!quote]`.

## Session routine

1. Check the manuscript hash (above).
2. Read `wiki/log.md` (last entries), `wiki/Rulings.md`, and `wiki/Open Questions.md`.
3. Do the work.
4. Run `bash tools/lint-links.sh` and `bash tools/build-index.sh`.
5. Append to `wiki/log.md`:

```
## [2026-10-03] build | first build of the wiki
```

Kinds: `build`, `ingest`, `ruling`, `query`, `lint`, `finding`.

6. Also file the Drive log the method requires: `LOGS/LOG_<date>_unownable-editor-steward.md`
   in the canonical LOGS folder (`17xDrstD-NFaQhZQScUeR7aldbvLo8pyZ`). Other stewards read
   Drive, not this repo.

## Publishing this wiki

`bash tools/build-github-wiki.sh repo` renders `gh-wiki/` with ordinary links you can browse in
the GitHub file view. `wiki --push` pushes to the repo's GitHub Wiki tab; that needs the Wiki
enabled, and on a private repo it needs a paid GitHub plan. **Never push while the repo is
public without Ammon's go in the same session.**
