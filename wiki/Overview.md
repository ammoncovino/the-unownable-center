# Overview

THE UNOWNABLE CENTER is a book by A.M. Covino on why systems built around something they cannot own (truth, God, the commons, the future) drift into treating it as property. **It is a separate book and a separate sale, not part of THE ALPHA-OMEGA WORKS** (Ammon, 30 September 2026).

This wiki is where its editor steward keeps the state of the job. It reads and counts the book; it does not edit it.

## Early findings

These come from reading only. Each goes to Ammon; none is fixed.

1. **The two drafts are one text in two orders.** The choice between them is order and what comes back, not which words. [[Two Drafts Compared]]
2. **The book's title idea is absent from its full prose.** "Unownable" appears only in the reconstructed Preface and Closing and one outline. [[The Unownable Center]]
3. **Thirteen promises the text does not keep**, including "This book began with the claim that peace is not the absence of conflict," which the Preface does not make. [[Promises the Text Does Not Keep]]
4. **Six terms used as if defined earlier**, all in Acceleration, all from outside the book. [[Undefined Terms]]
5. **The Lord's Prayer section is an outline the rest of the book leans on.** [[The Lord's Prayer as Governor]]
6. **Red Words Inversion is named three times and does not exist.** [[Red Words Inversion]]
