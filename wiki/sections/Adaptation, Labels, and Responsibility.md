## What it says

- "A nervous system is built under conditions it did not choose. It adapts to those conditions as best it can."
- Labels (ADHD, OCD, trauma) are useful until "a label stops being a description and becomes a verdict."
- The sentence it offers instead: "This is the system I have. This is how it learned to stay alive. Given that, what do I do now?"
- Mutual care "is one of the things evolution produced."
- Closes: "You do not control how your system was assembled. You are still the one steering it now."
