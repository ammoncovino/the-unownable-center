## What it says

The people "cannot tolerate an unownable center for long"; they compress unseen authority into an object that "can be carried, guarded, ritualized, and administered"; this is "the first major political-theological form of enclosure." The wider error is "confusing a symbol with the reality it was meant to point toward." See [[Enclosure]], [[Compression]].
