## What it says

- What the book is not: "doctrine, deconversion, partisan argument, or prophecy." What it is: "a structural reading of systems."
- The method, as questions: what does a system reward, hide, compress, call reality, and what happens "when the model stops matching the world?"
- The test cases it promises: "Scripture, constitutions, empires, arenas, platforms, AI, and personal identity."
- The governing claim (line 19): every durable system builds itself around a center "it did not create and cannot finally own." Empires call it order, religions God, markets value, platforms engagement, AI labs intelligence. "Crisis begins when an institution mistakes stewardship for possession." See [[The Unownable Center]].
