# Compression

Reducing something large (experience, authority, behavior) into something small that can be carried and used: a story, an object, a score, a model. Useful, and always lossy.

> [!quote] Acceleration, line 736
> "Every model is a compression of reality. The compression is useful — that is why models exist. But the compression is also lossy."
