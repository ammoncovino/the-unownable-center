# Drift

A system slowly coming to serve its own survival instead of the problem it was built for, usually without anyone intending it.

> [!quote] Acceleration, lines 652 to 657
> "1. A real problem was identified. 2. A structure was built to address it. 3. The structure accumulated power. 4. The structure began to serve its own continuity rather than the original problem. 5. Narrative was deployed to justify the drift. 6. The drift was invisible to participants until exposure occurred."

> [!quote] Acceleration, line 724
> "Drift without malice — the central operational insight of this framework"
