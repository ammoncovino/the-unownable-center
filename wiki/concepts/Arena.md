# Arena

A constrained environment, designed by someone else, where people compete under rules they did not set while others watch.

> [!quote] Arena Stories, line 370
> "A group of people is placed in a constrained environment. Rules are imposed from above. Spectators watch. Outcomes are shaped by both individual decisions and an invisible structure."
