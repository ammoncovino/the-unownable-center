# Interface

The layer of a system that tells its users what to notice and what to ignore.

> [!quote] The Red Words as a System, line 112
> "Every complex system has an interface, a layer where the user is told what to pay attention to and what to ignore. A software dashboard does this with charts and warnings. A financial statement does it with categories and totals. The red sayings do it with images."
