# Governor

A repeated form that limits what a person, a government, or a machine can claim, take, or demand. In engineering a governor is the part that stops an engine running faster than it should; the book borrows that sense.

> [!quote] The Lord's Prayer as Governor, line 37
> "a sacred text functioning as a governor: a repeated form that limits what the speaker can claim, accumulate, or demand."
