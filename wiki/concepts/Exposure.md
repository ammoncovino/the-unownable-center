# Exposure

The moment a system's hidden structure becomes visible, usually because its model of the world stopped matching the world.

> [!quote] Gog and Magog, Final Exposure, line 560
> "at some point, the mismatch between how systems see themselves and how the world actually works will be too large to hide. That is what exposure feels like from the inside."
