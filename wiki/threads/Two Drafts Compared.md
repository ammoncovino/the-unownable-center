# Two Drafts Compared

There are two drafts of this book. They carry the same prose. They differ in order, in what they leave out, and in two placeholder chapters.

> [!note] How this was checked
> Both files were split at their top-level headings and compared section by section, word by word, after removing recovery comments and markdown escapes. Run on 3 October 2026 against `manuscript/complete-recovered-draft.md` and `raw/reading-draft_8Aug2026.md`.

## The result

Every full-prose section is **word for word identical** in both drafts (similarity 1.000): the Preface, The Red Words as a System, When Models Become Gods, Adaptation, Arena Stories, Reddit as Arena, Gethsemane, Crucifixion and Resurrection, Gog and Magog, and the Closing Reflection. Acceleration and the appendix differ only in headings and one label line (0.998 and 0.991).

**So the choice between the drafts is not a choice between two texts.** It is a choice of order, of whether the eleven outlines stay, and of whether the two new placeholder chapters are written.
