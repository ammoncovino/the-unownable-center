## What the records say

> [!quote] Reading draft, Editorial Note, 8 August
> "That volume does not exist. The Governor Prompt that would produce it has been run on 1 of 56 passages."

So: **GI-011** is presumably passage 11 of 56 in a governor-index numbering. The text does not say so; that is a reading of the label, not a fact.

## The Governor Index, across books

The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Homo Sapiens has a section, "What the Red Words Say About Rulers," built on what it calls the Governor Index: six sayings about authority set beside an ordinary and an inverted reading. Its history, from Grok's audit of 30 September:

| When | Where the Guide says the full reading lives |
|---|---|
| Older draft (repo `books/hitchhikers-guide.md`) | "its own book, The Unownable Center" |
| Shipping v2.6, 24 Sept | "in The Lens" |
| The Lens v1.9 | contains no red-words or Governor Index material |

So the series points readers at a reading no shipping book contains, and this book points at a volume that does not exist. That is the series' problem and Ammon's ruling, not this steward's to fix. It matters here because **this book is the nearest thing to a home that reading has.**
