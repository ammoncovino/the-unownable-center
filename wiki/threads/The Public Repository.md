# The Public Repository

This repository, `ammoncovino/the-unownable-center`, is **public**. Anyone who finds it can read the recovered draft. As of 30 September it was the only place the book could be read publicly. The site carries nothing.

## Two records that pull different ways

> [!quote] LICENSE.md, 21 June 2026
> "The author's stated intent for this project is public reading, free sharing, and non-commercial educational use, while retaining copyright"

> [!quote] Ammon, 22 September, in substance (handoff, section 3.2)
> The Unownable Center is not for sale and not anywhere on the website until it is ready.

The 22 September ruling names the website and the store; it does not name GitHub. So the public repo may not break it in letter. It does mean the draft is readable while the book is not ready.

## What happens to this wiki

The wiki holds the handoff, the open questions, the rulings, and notes about Ammon's own unwritten chapter. **None of it is pushed to this repo while the repo is public, without Ammon's go in the same session.** See [[Open Questions]], G0.

> [!note] Teaching note: what "private" changes
> Making a GitHub repo private hides it from everyone but Ammon and people he invites. Nothing in it is lost, and the nightly backup keeps working. GitHub's Wiki tab on a private repo needs a paid plan, but this wiki also lives as ordinary files in `wiki/`, readable in the repo's file view on any plan, which is how the series wiki works.
