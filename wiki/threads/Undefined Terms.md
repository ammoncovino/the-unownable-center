# Undefined Terms

Names the book uses as if a reader already knows them. None is defined anywhere in either draft. Several are Ammon's terms from other work; whether they come into this book, get defined here, or come out is his ruling.

| Term | Line | What the text assumes |
|---|---|---|
| The Signal Chain | 608 | "defined earlier in this framework" |
| "this framework" | 608, 638, 669, 702, 724, 728 | A framework outside the book. Line 724 calls drift without malice its "central operational insight." |
| Alpha, Prescriptive, Omega modes | 624, 682 to 688 | "The Alpha-Omega framework describes three modes of reasoning." See [[Alpha-Omega References]]. |
| The LIFE system | 671 | "defined in the LIFE system as a bounded AI tool" |
| The Constrained Epistemic Instrument | 667 to 680 | Named as a heading and defined in four rules, but attributed to the LIFE system. |
| The Prophecy Trap | 663, 714 | "the Prophecy Trap as scripted escalation." Not a section in either draft. |
| The Structural Lens and its five signals | 704 | "The same five signals that identify drift in human content." Five are listed in one sentence. |
| The Governor Prompt, GI-011 | 196 | See [[Red Words Inversion]]. |
| The Alpha to Omega diamond | 175 | See [[Alpha-Omega References]]. |
| The Bolt Rule | reading draft only, line 144 | "Any inversion that dissolves identity must be followed by an action that carries responsibility without reclaiming identity." Quoted in the placeholder as something to state; not in the recovered draft at all. |

> [!quote] The reading draft's own editorial note, 8 August
> "'Acceleration' cites six things that do not exist... None are defined in this draft, and none appear in 13,495 lines of the inversion transcript. A reader hits 'defined earlier' and finds no earlier. Define each in a paragraph, or cut the back-references."

> [!note] Teaching note
> A back-reference that points at nothing is one of the most common defects in assembled books. It happens when a chapter is written inside a larger body of work and then moved. Acceleration reads like it was written for the series and then placed here.
