THE GLASS is Chapter 8 of THE ALPHA-OMEGA LENS and the opening of that book. Ammon ruled on 30 September that it should also ship as a free reader. Grok's audit of 30 September found **three different texts called The Glass**: a 388-word analogy, the 683-word Lens chapter, and a roughly 1,700-word free-entry document. Which one Ammon means is open in the series' own work, not in this book's.
