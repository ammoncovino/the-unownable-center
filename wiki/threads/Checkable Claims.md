# Checkable Claims

Where the manuscript states something as fact about the world that a reader could check. **This page locates them. It does not judge them.** Each needs a source, a limit stated in the open, or a mark that it is the author's reading. That work is the hedge register and the sources pass (handoff, steps 8 and 9), after the scope rulings. The text has no notes and no citations.

> [!note] Teaching note: CARRY, SOFTEN, BLOCK
> The hedge register sorts claims three ways. CARRY: the claim stays, with its limit stated where the reader sees it (most readings of scripture will be this). SOFTEN: the claim stays, worded down to what the evidence holds. BLOCK: the claim cannot ship as worded. **None of those sorts is made here.**
