## What it sets out

- The self is "a sequence of changing states linked by continuity" (section 1).
- The loop: "Signal → Perception → Interpretation → Response → Feedback → Update" (section 2). This is probably what Acceleration calls "the Signal Chain defined earlier," but the appendix does not use that name and comes after Acceleration, not before. See [[Undefined Terms]].
- Humans add a narrative layer, "System failure in humans typically originates in this layer" (section 6).
- A receiving mind rejects what destabilizes it faster than it can adapt, "not based on truth value" (section 10).
- So transmission needs a core layer that does not change and an [[Interface]] layer that does (sections 13 to 16).

> [!note] Teaching note: why this matters for the book
> Sections 13 to 16 describe what a book like this one has to do: keep the argument fixed and adjust the entry point, order, and intensity for the reader. In that sense the appendix is the book's own theory of how it should be written. Whether it belongs in the book or behind it is Ammon's ruling.
